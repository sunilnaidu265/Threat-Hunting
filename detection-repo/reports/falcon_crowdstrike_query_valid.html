Command-line Abuse CommandLine contains "npm install" OR "npm i " OR "npm ci" OR "pnpm install" OR "yarn install" OR "postinstall" OR "preinstall" OR "install.js" Flags package installation and lifecycle hook abuse.
Execution / Download CommandLine contains "powershell" OR "cmd.exe" OR "curl " OR "wget " OR "base64" Captures script execution and payload retrieval activity.
Node Malware Signals CommandLine contains "eval(" OR "child_process" OR "execSync" OR "spawn(" OR "registry.npmjs.org" OR ".npmrc" OR "package-lock.json" OR "node_modules" OR "npmrc" Detects JavaScript execution patterns and registry/package abuse.
